# Release 2.1 (provisional)

Three targeted corrections requested after researcher review.

## 1. Historical asterisk notes
An exhaustive scan of the Appendix found exactly two historical source-note types, `(*)` and `(**)`, both in Protocol II. Their note bodies were verified visually on PDF pp. 208-209 and separated from the running text. Every other naked asterisk returned by OCR was checked visually and proved to be an OCR/layout artifact, usually a superscript apparatus number. Witness-specific readings are represented provisionally from De Michelis apparatus 351-358.

## 2. Protocol segmentation
The reconstructed text was resegmented from the printed protocol headings rather than from the position of apparatus-note anchors. This fixes the recurrent error in which the beginning of a protocol (and therefore its punctuation/syntax) had been left at the end of the preceding protocol. The TEI div boundaries and web reader were updated accordingly.

## 3. Span-level K provenance
The reader now contains a word-span provenance layer comparing the De Michelis reconstruction with the current apparatus-derived K reconstruction. It distinguishes: text agreeing with K, text with no corresponding K tokens, and readings differing from K. This is explicitly provisional and is not a substitute for collation against the Znamja scans.

There is no single global point at which De Michelis says K ceases to exist: he describes cuts distributed across the text, especially in Protocols 12-22. The temporary provenance layer therefore marks local spans rather than inventing a single cut-off.
